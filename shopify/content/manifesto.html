There is a life you can see that does not exist yet.

Something you want to build. Someone you want to become. Something you know you need to do.

The distance between seeing it and creating it is courage.

  • Courage to fail.
  • Courage to be misunderstood.
  • Courage to be disliked.
  • Courage to leave what is comfortable.
  • Courage to act before you know how it ends.

We believe everything is possible. But possibility means nothing without the courage to act.

Vates exists for that moment.

Vates — for the creators of the world.
