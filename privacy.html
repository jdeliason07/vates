Privacy

Last updated 18 August 2026

The short version: we collect what is needed to send you a bottle and to work out which advert or creator brought you here. We do not sell anything about you to anyone.

What the storefront itself stores

This site has no accounts, no server and no database. What it keeps lives in your own browser:

  • Where you came from — the campaign or creator tag on the link you arrived through, plus the referring site. Kept so a sale can be credited to whoever earned it.
  • Whether a confirmation has already been counted, so a refresh does not report the same order twice.

Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

Payment

Payment is taken by Stripe, on Stripe's own pages. Your card details are never sent to this site and we never see them. Stripe receives your name, email, and billing and shipping addresses in order to take the payment, send the receipt and let us post the parcel. Stripe's handling of it is covered by Stripe's privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

Depending on which campaigns are running, this site may load measurement tags from Meta, TikTok and Google. They use cookies and similar identifiers to tell the platform that a visit or a purchase followed one of their adverts, which is what makes advertising possible to measure at all. They are subject to those companies' own privacy policies.

You can refuse them: browser tracking protection, an extension, or the advertising controls inside your Meta, TikTok and Google accounts all work, and none of them stop you buying or being sent a bottle.

Email

Your receipt is sent by Stripe because you bought something. We do not add you to a mailing list off the back of an order.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, EU, or a US state with its own privacy statute, you can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Reply to your receipt and ask. Deleting an order record may mean we can no longer honour a return on it, so we will say so before doing it.

Children

This shop is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
