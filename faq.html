Questions

Everything here is answered from what the shop actually does. Where a figure is not settled yet, it says so rather than guessing.

The bottle

What is No. 01?

A one-piece borosilicate glass water bottle with a machined steel cap, 32 oz — 950 ml. One piece of glass means no seam and no plastic liner; the cap's threads are cut rather than moulded. It is $89.

What comes in the box?

The bottle, and the four things that go with it:

  • A notepad — unruled, 60 pages.
  • A pen — one per box.
  • The “Page of Inspiration” — one sheet.
  • A welcome letter, numbered by hand.

None of the four is sold on its own. They are in every box at the one price.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Yes — bottle and cap both. Borosilicate is the glass laboratory glassware is made of; it takes the heat and the thermal swing.

How tall is it, and how much does it weigh?

Not published yet. Those two figures come off the spec sheet for the production run, and until that sheet is final they sit as dashes on the In hand panel rather than as numbers somebody estimated. The capacity — 32 oz, 950 ml — is settled.

When is the clear finish coming?

No date. Smoke is the finish that ships; clear is drawn on the shop page marked “Coming soon”, with no price, because there isn't one to quote yet.

What does “an edition of 500” mean?

The run is capped at 500 bottles and each one is numbered by hand. There is no counter ticking down on this site — a figure nobody can verify is fabricated scarcity, so the page carries the cap and nothing else until real inventory can back a number.

Ordering

How do I pay?

Checkout is Stripe. Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link. There is no account to make and no cart page in between — Purchase goes straight there.

Stripe's receipt email is your order confirmation. Keep it; replying to it is the fastest way to reach us about the order.

Is tax included in the $89?

No. Sales tax, where it applies, is calculated at checkout. Shipping is free, so tax is the only thing that can be added.

Can I cancel or change an order?

If the parcel has not gone out yet, we can cancel it outright. Reply to your receipt and say so. Once it has shipped it becomes a return, which costs you nothing either.

Shipping

What does shipping cost, and how long does it take?

Shipping is free, everywhere we ship. Orders leave within two business days. Domestic delivery is typically three to five business days from dispatch; international is seven to twenty-one, depending on the destination and its customs.

The full terms are on the shipping page.

How do I track it?

You get a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves us. If it has been more than ten business days domestically, or thirty internationally, tell us and we will replace it — we won't ask you to wait it out.

Will I be charged duties?

Possibly, on an order crossing a border. Import duties are set by the destination country and are the recipient's to pay. We cannot calculate them in advance or absorb them.

Returns

What is the return policy?

Thirty days from delivery, for any reason or none. The bottle needs to be in a condition you would be content to receive. Return shipping is on us — ask for a label and we will send one.

When do I get the money back?

Refunds go back to the original payment method within five business days of the bottle reaching us. Stripe emails you a refund notice when it is issued; how quickly it lands after that is your bank's business, not ours.

It arrived broken.

It is glass, and couriers are couriers. Send a photograph and we ship a replacement the same day. Don't bother returning the pieces, and don't wait for the replacement before telling us.

Us

How do I reach a person?

Reply to your receipt. It reaches the same place as anything else and arrives with your order attached, which makes every answer faster. There is no phone line and no chat widget pretending to be staffed.

Why aren't there any reviews?

Because No. 01 has not shipped to anyone yet, so there is nothing honest to quote. The cards on the shop page carry sourced lines from the archive — each printed with where it came from — and say plainly underneath that they are not customer reviews. They get swapped for real ones the day there are real ones.

Can I sell it with you?

Yes. What each creator has earned is public on the leaderboard. Say hello on Instagram to start.
